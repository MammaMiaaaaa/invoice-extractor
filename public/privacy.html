Privacy Policy – Invoice Extractor

Last updated: 23 September 2026

What we collect

When you send a message to our WhatsApp number we receive your WhatsApp phone number, the message, and any PDF invoice you attach.

How we use it

Invoice PDFs are sent to Google Gemini to extract invoice fields (date, supplier, tax ID, amounts). The extracted data is saved to a private Google Sheet and shown in an internal web form. We reply to you on WhatsApp with a summary. We use your data only to process the invoices you send.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is processed only by the service providers needed to run the service: Meta (WhatsApp), Google (Gemini and Google Sheets).

Retention and deletion

Invoice data is kept in the Google Sheet until the business that operates this service deletes it. To ask for your data to be deleted, send "delete my data" to the same WhatsApp number.
